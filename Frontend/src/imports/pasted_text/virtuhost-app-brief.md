Design and prototype a premium mobile app called “VirtuHost” — an AI-powered listing and property management platform for Airbnb and short-term rental hosts.

CORE PRODUCT IDEA

VirtuHost allows property owners and hosts to manage their entire short-term rental operation from one mobile app.

The defining feature is an intelligent AI hosting assistant that can actually perform actions on behalf of the host, not just answer questions.

A host should be able to type or speak natural-language commands such as:

“Who is checking in today?”
“Reply to Sarah and tell her early check-in is available from 1 PM.”
“Block this weekend for maintenance.”
“Change the nightly price to R1,450 next Friday.”
“Add these photos to the Sandton apartment.”
“Show me my revenue this month.”
“Which guests haven’t received check-in instructions?”
“Send check-in details to tomorrow’s guests.”
“Create a 10% discount for the next two weeks.”
“Update the Wi-Fi password across all guest messages.”

The AI should understand the request, access the relevant listing/bookings/messages/settings, propose or perform the action, and then show a clean confirmation card.

DESIGN DIRECTION

Create a sophisticated, ultra-clean mobile UI inspired by premium modern AI applications, high-end travel products, and contemporary editorial product design.

The supplied reference image should influence the overall level of:
- visual smoothness
- whitespace
- rounded geometry
- typography
- floating controls
- subtle shadows
- minimal navigation
- AI-first interaction
- premium presentation

Do NOT simply copy the reference interface.

Develop a distinct VirtuHost visual identity.

The app should feel like:
luxury hospitality × intelligent software × calm property management.

Avoid the typical cluttered SaaS dashboard aesthetic.

Use:
- warm white / soft off-white backgrounds
- near-black typography
- very subtle cool grey surfaces
- restrained purple/lilac accent colour
- occasional soft gradients
- large rounded cards
- thin borders
- subtle shadows and depth
- generous spacing
- beautiful property photography
- smooth transitions
- sophisticated micro-interactions

Typography should feel similar to modern Swiss/product design:
large bold headlines, compact labels, excellent hierarchy and highly readable body text.

Use realistic content throughout the application instead of lorem ipsum.

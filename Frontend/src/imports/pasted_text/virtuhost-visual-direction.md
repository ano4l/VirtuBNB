VISUAL DIRECTION — EXTREMELY IMPORTANT

VirtuHost should feel unmistakably premium, futuristic and Apple-like.

Think:
Apple iOS 26 design language
Apple Home
Apple Wallet
Apple Intelligence
visionOS
high-end Apple product pages
premium hospitality photography

The interface should feel like it could be a native first-party Apple application designed specifically for property management.

The UI must be EXTREMELY CLEAN.

Do not create a conventional SaaS dashboard.
Do not overuse cards.
Do not fill every empty space.
Do not use heavy borders.
Do not create dense grids of statistics.
Do not make the interface look like a generic startup template.

Whitespace is a major design element.

LIQUID GLASS

Use a restrained interpretation of Apple's Liquid Glass aesthetic throughout the interface.

Use translucent floating glass surfaces for:
- bottom navigation
- AI command bar
- contextual controls
- filters
- floating action buttons
- confirmation sheets
- AI action cards
- selected calendar controls

Glass surfaces should have:
- strong background blur
- subtle refraction
- extremely soft borders
- realistic transparency
- slight internal highlights
- delicate shadows
- rounded continuous corners
- layered depth

The glass should react visually to the content underneath it.

Do NOT make every component glass.

Most of the interface should remain beautifully simple and almost flat, allowing the glass elements to feel special.

SHADERS & AMBIENT VISUALS

Introduce subtle premium shader effects to represent VirtuHost intelligence.

Create extremely soft abstract gradients and shader fields using:

icy white
silver
very pale lavender
soft violet
subtle blue
occasional warmer hospitality tones

Shaders should resemble:
light passing through glass,
soft aurora,
refracted light,
fluid gradients,
diffused volumetric light.

Never use loud neon gradients.

The shaders should almost disappear into the white background.

Use them especially around the Virtu AI experience.

When Virtu is thinking, listening or performing an action, the ambient shader can gently animate or shift.

AI PRESENCE

Avoid the typical “purple AI chatbot” aesthetic.

Virtu AI should feel more like an intelligent layer living inside the operating system.

Represent AI using:
- light
- movement
- subtle shaders
- glass
- spatial depth
- elegant animation

Rather than a robot icon.

The main AI orb/button can appear as a small piece of refractive glass containing a slowly moving lavender/blue light field.

When idle:
very subtle movement.

When listening:
glass softly expands and the internal shader reacts to voice.

When thinking:
light slowly flows through the glass.

When executing:
a brief elegant pulse.

When completed:
the shader resolves back into a calm neutral state.

HOME SCREEN

Make the Home screen dramatically simpler.

Large header:

Good morning, Anotida.

Underneath:

Tuesday, 9 September

Then a natural-language operational summary:

“2 guests are arriving today.
Everything is ready.”

Do NOT immediately show a grid of dashboard cards.

Below this should be the hero AI interaction.

Large floating glass command surface:

“What can I take care of?”

Include a subtle animated AI shader/orb and microphone.

Below it, show only the most important information.

For example:

Today

14:00
Sarah is checking in
Sandton City Apartment

10:00
Michael is checking out
Rosebank Loft

Allow the content to breathe.

AI HOME EXPERIENCE

The AI assistant should be accessible from almost everywhere.

When activated, do not always navigate to a completely separate chatbot page.

Allow the AI interface to smoothly expand upward from the floating command bar into a beautiful glass conversation surface.

This should feel spatial.

The background interface subtly blurs and recedes.

The AI layer floats above it.

Example:

User:
“What's happening today?”

Virtu:

“Pretty quiet.

Sarah arrives at Sandton City Apartment at 14:00.

Michael checked out of Rosebank Loft this morning and cleaning is scheduled for 11:30.

No guest messages need your attention.”

Then show:

[View Sarah's booking]

Keep responses concise and human.

LISTINGS

Listings should feel closer to Apple Photos than a property management CRM.

Use beautiful large photography.

Property cards should have minimal information.

Sandton City Apartment

Sandton
4.92 ★

R1,450 / night

Do not surround every property with visible borders.

Allow photography and typography to create hierarchy.

Opening a property should use a smooth image expansion transition.

PROPERTY SCREEN

Use a large edge-to-edge hero image.

Overlay minimal glass controls over the photography.

Below:

Sandton City Apartment

Sandton, Johannesburg

4.92 ★   87 reviews

Then simple horizontally scrolling sections:

Overview
Calendar
Pricing
Gallery
Details

Avoid displaying everything simultaneously.

AI should remain available through a small floating glass button.

The host could simply say:

“Make this R1,700 this weekend.”

Virtu understands that “this” refers to the currently opened property.

MESSAGES

Messages should feel similar to a premium native messaging application.

Very little chrome.

Large typography.
Generous whitespace.
Natural gestures.

When a guest sends:

“Hi, would it be possible to check in early?”

Show a tiny contextual Virtu suggestion:

Reply with Virtu

Tapping it generates a response using:
booking information
availability
property rules
previous messages

The host can edit before sending.

CALENDAR

The calendar should feel extremely tactile.

Large clean dates.

Very subtle visual indicators for:

Booked
Available
Blocked

Selecting dates causes a floating Liquid Glass toolbar to appear:

Price
Availability
Discount
Ask Virtu

Avoid permanently displaying excessive controls.

ANIMATION

Animation quality is extremely important.

Use:
spring animations
soft scaling
blur transitions
glass morphing
matched geometry transitions
subtle parallax
shader movement
interactive drag gestures

Navigation should feel physical and spatial.

Elements should morph between states rather than simply disappearing and reappearing.

Example:

The AI orb in the bottom navigation should physically morph into the AI command bar.

The command bar should morph into the conversation interface.

A property thumbnail should expand into the property's hero image.

An AI action card should morph into a success confirmation.

BOTTOM NAVIGATION

Create an extremely minimal floating Liquid Glass navigation dock.

Icons:

Home
Bookings
Virtu AI
Messages
Listings

Do not permanently show all text labels.

Use simple SF Symbols-style line icons.

The currently selected section can receive a subtle glass highlight.

Make Virtu AI the central interaction.

It may appear as a small refractive orb rather than a standard navigation icon.

COLOUR

Primary background:
#F8F8F6 / warm Apple-like white

Primary text:
near black

Secondary:
soft graphite

Accent:
restrained lavender / violet

Use colour extremely sparingly.

Most screens should visually consist of:
white
black
photography
glass
light

The AI shader provides the primary colour expression.

DARK MODE

Also design a beautiful dark appearance.

Near-black backgrounds rather than pure black.

Glass becomes smoked/translucent.

AI shaders become slightly more visible against the dark environment.

Property photography should remain the dominant visual element.

OVERALL FEELING

VirtuHost should feel:

quiet
intelligent
expensive
effortless
spatial
trustworthy
native
alive

The user should never feel like they are operating complicated property-management software.

The experience should feel like they simply tell Virtu what they want done.

The core product philosophy is:

“Don't manage your Airbnb.
Tell Virtu what needs to happen.”

Design every screen around that philosophy.
